## Known evidence gaps and safety gates

- The deployed source snapshot does **not** prove legal/provider acceptance, complete QR generation, a billing provider, or functional Portal Settings fields. These cannot be reported as complete.
- Deployed `FbrInvoicesController` logs raw request/payload/provider data; V2 must not copy that behavior. Company/FBR credentials must remain encrypted and absent from resources, audit metadata and logs.
- Later edits to Company, buyer/customer, settings or FBR configuration must not rewrite a submitted document. Current V2 now retains first-claim seller fields, but lacks a complete immutable branding/print snapshot; no complete print-parity claim is made.
- Native Accounting and dedicated FBR Invoice must remain independent. No historical import or live FBR operation may create journals, AR, customer payments, inventory movement or banking effects by implication.
