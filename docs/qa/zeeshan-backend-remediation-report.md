## Boundaries and unresolved items

Native Accounting invoices and FBR Invoicing remain separate models, routes, and lifecycles. This candidate does not alter either FBR submission transport or financial posting. No historical or live FBR invoice becomes an accounting invoice. No automatic journal, AR, payroll, attendance, leave, banking, inventory, or CRM effect was added.

Open decisions are the full sensitive payroll bank-account contract (#5), authoritative HS-code catalogue (#19), and a separately approved overnight-shift design (#10). DEV-specific authentication symptoms (#2, #13, #14, #22) require redacted request/response and configuration evidence; they are not grounds to weaken membership or explicit employee-link requirements.

Local verification and external MariaDB results must be recorded separately in the final handoff; this report does not assert unexecuted certification.
